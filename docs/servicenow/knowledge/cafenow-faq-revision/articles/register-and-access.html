CafeNow by NowServing Solutions connects employees and cafeteria vendors through the workplace Service Portal.

  1. Use the CafeNow portal address supplied by your organization or instructor.
  2. Sign in with your assigned account using the configured sign-in method.
  3. Open the menu to browse vendors. Your assigned roles determine which employee, vendor and administration tools are available.
  4. If access is missing, ask the CafeNow administrator to check your account, role and group assignments.

An account does not automatically grant vendor or administration access. Contact the administrator for account provisioning; do not assume self-registration or a particular approval process is available.

Do not share login credentials. If sign-in fails, verify your username and use your organization's approved password-assistance process. Ask the administrator to check whether the account is active or locked.
