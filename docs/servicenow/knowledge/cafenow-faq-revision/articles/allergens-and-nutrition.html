Nutrition, ingredient and allergen information is supplied by vendors where available. CafeNow does not independently verify food safety and cannot guarantee an item is allergen-free.

  1. Review the available information for the food item before ordering.
  2. Confirm ingredients, substitutions and possible cross-contact directly with the vendor.
  3. Ask about shared utensils, equipment and preparation areas.
  4. If the vendor cannot confirm the information you need, do not assume the item meets your dietary requirements.
  5. Report missing or incorrect information to the vendor and CafeNow support.

Common ingredients and allergens to ask about include milk, eggs, wheat and gluten-containing ingredients, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, sesame, fish, shellfish and mustard. This is not an exhaustive list.

Nutrition values are estimates and may vary with portion size, recipe and preparation. Missing information is not a safety assurance.
