1. Open My Orders in the CafeNow portal.
  2. Find your order by its order number and open its details.
  3. Review the vendor, items, pickup details and current status.
  4. Refresh the page or use Refresh status where shown to obtain the latest saved status.

Order statuses

Submitted: the order has been recorded and awaits vendor acknowledgement. Confirmed: the vendor has acknowledged the order. Ready for Pickup: the order is ready to collect. Closed Complete: the order was collected. Canceled: the order was canceled.

Status refresh is manual in the prepared employee views. Check My Orders rather than relying on automatic email or real-time updates. If the order is late, contact the vendor with your order number.
