1. Open a vendor menu.
  2. Select a whole-number quantity for an available item and add it to the cart.
  3. Add other foods from that same vendor.
  4. Open the full cart to review quantities, unit prices, item subtotals and the order total.
  5. Adjust or remove items before choosing a pickup window and submitting.

Each order is limited to one vendor. Finish or clear the current cart before ordering from another vendor. Adding food to the cart does not place an order or guarantee availability.
