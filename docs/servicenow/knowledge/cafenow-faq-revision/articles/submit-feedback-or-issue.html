1. For a question about a food item, ingredients or pickup, contact the responsible vendor.
  2. For an order or portal issue, open Complaints and Requests when available to your role.
  3. Select the submission type and category. Provide a clear summary and details, including the vendor and order number when relevant.
  4. Submit once and check the confirmation or error message. If access is denied, ask the CafeNow administrator to review it.

Do not include passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Reported issues do not automatically cancel an order, issue a refund or prove a notification was delivered.

For urgent food allergy or food-safety concerns, contact the responsible vendor directly rather than relying on a feedback submission.

The prepared support form is enabled for CafeNow requesters. Vendor-only submission remains pending setup; vendor staff should use their agreed administrator/support contact if the form is not available.
