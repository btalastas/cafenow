1. Open Menu in the CafeNow portal.
  2. Choose a vendor and review its available menu items.
  3. Review the item description, price and any vendor-provided ingredient, nutrition or allergen information.
  4. Choose the quantity and add the item to your cart. Keep one vendor per order.

Availability and prices may change before checkout. If an item is unavailable, choose an alternative or ask the vendor. Missing nutrition or allergen details do not mean an item is safe for a particular allergy.
