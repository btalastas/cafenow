Cancellation policy applies to your own orders and closes 20 minutes before the pickup-window start. Eligible order states and exact cutoff-boundary behavior must be confirmed with the vendor or CafeNow administrator.

  1. Open My Orders and identify the order and its pickup window.
  2. Check whether the portal provides an authorized cancellation action for this order.
  3. If no cancellation action is available, contact the vendor or support promptly with the order number; do not assume a support request automatically cancels the order.
  4. Confirm the order actually shows Canceled before treating the cancellation as complete.

Employees cannot use status fields to advance vendor fulfillment. The pilot has no payment/refund processing. Contacting support after the cutoff does not guarantee cancellation.
