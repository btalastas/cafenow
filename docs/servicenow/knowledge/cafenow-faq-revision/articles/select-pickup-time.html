1. Add food from one vendor and open the full cart.
  2. Choose one pickup window offered for that vendor.
  3. Review the selected window and the ordering terms before placing the order.
  4. Check the saved pickup details in My Orders and collect during the selected window when the order is Ready for Pickup.

Ordering policy closes ordering 20 minutes before the pickup-window start. For example, an 11:30 AM start has an 11:10 AM ordering cutoff. Plan to submit before the cutoff.

A visible window is not a capacity reservation. If the desired window is unavailable, select another offered window or contact the vendor. Do not assume that a recurring time-window label independently confirms a service date; ask the vendor if the date is unclear.
