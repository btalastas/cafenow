Use CafeNow to select food from one vendor, choose a pickup window, and track your order.

  1. Browse the available vendors and menu items.
  2. Add foods and quantities from one vendor to your cart.
  3. Review prices, select the vendor pickup window and read the terms before submitting.
  4. After successful submission, keep the order number and check My Orders.
  5. Collect the order when it is Ready for Pickup during the selected window.

The class pilot uses simulated checkout. No payment is collected through this ordering journey.
