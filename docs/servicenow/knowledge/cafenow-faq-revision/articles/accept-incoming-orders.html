1. Open the vendor order queue and choose the relevant pickup window.
  2. Review each order and its items before acknowledging it.
  3. Confirm eligible Submitted orders using the authorized vendor action.
  4. If batch confirmation is available, review which orders succeeded, were skipped or failed; do not assume every selected order changed.
  5. Refresh the queue to see current saved states, especially after another vendor user changes an order.

Act only on your own vendor's orders. Do not confirm an order that is already canceled, and do not repeatedly apply an action because the screen has not refreshed.

The planned capacity is 20 orders per vendor pickup window, not 20 meals. The simplified ordering service has not established simultaneous-booking protection; administrators must verify capacity enforcement before relying on that limit.

If food cannot be supplied, follow the agreed vendor exception process and record a reason for any authorized vendor cancellation. Do not invent a replacement state or mark an uncollected order complete.
