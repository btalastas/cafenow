CafeNow vendor tools are intended for assigned vendor administrators and vendor staff. Access must remain limited to the vendor records associated with your authorized vendor group.

  1. Sign in using your assigned vendor account.
  2. Open the vendor tools available in your CafeNow portal.
  3. Review incoming orders and their pickup windows.
  4. Open an order to review all items, quantities and any available instructions.
  5. Confirm eligible orders, mark them Ready for Pickup when ready, and complete them only after collection.
  6. Use menu maintenance according to your role: vendor administrators can maintain menu items in the prepared widget; vendor staff have read-only menu access.

If no vendor is found or another vendor's records appear, contact the administrator. Do not work around access restrictions or use an employee account to process vendor orders.
