CafeNow by NowServing Solutions is a one-building, multi-vendor class pilot. Facility managers handle the business-administration information authorized for them; CafeNow administrators manage the application within their granted permissions. Business roles do not automatically grant platform administration.

Access and knowledge

Employees use the CafeNow user role; vendors use vendor admin/staff roles; facility managers use the facility manager role. CafeNow admins see all FAQ categories. Multiple personas combine visible categories. Apply knowledge-base and article user criteria separately: hiding a category does not protect direct article links, search results or attachments.

Ordering and operations

The current path is Vendor/Menu -> custom cart and pickup selection -> one authoritative CafeNow Order with Order Lines. Do not create a second fulfillment authority from historical Catalog Item/Request/RITM examples. Payments and refunds remain outside this pilot.

Recorded states are Submitted, Confirmed, Ready for Pickup, Closed Complete and Canceled. Closed Complete means actual pickup. Ordering policy is 20 minutes before window start; cancellation policy is also 20 minutes, with eligible states and exact-boundary behavior still to confirm.

Verification priorities

  1. Verify employee ownership and vendor isolation through portal pages, native records, direct URLs and APIs.
  2. Verify server-side cutoff, capacity and duplicate-submission handling before treating these as enforced. The simplified local checkout does not enforce cutoff or capacity and has no durable duplicate-request identity.
  3. Reconcile orders, lines and totals after failures or uncertain responses before advising a resubmission.
  4. Check saved state transitions and actual notification/email records. Prepared reminder text is not proof of automated delivery.
  5. Verify requester complaint submission and vendor support routing independently; vendor-only complaint submission remains pending configuration.
  6. Verify the FAQ/About pages, header/footer links and any fixed-footer clearance as each persona.

The three-notice no-show direction has unresolved scheduling details. Do not infer a disposal time or automatically complete uncollected orders. Keep credentials out of project files and record evidence and unresolved issues in the agreed project tracking locations.
