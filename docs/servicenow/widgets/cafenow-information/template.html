1. Overview

CafeNow provides employees with access to nutrition and allergen information for food and beverages offered by participating cafeteria vendors.

Our goal is to help employees make informed food choices based on their dietary preferences, nutritional requirements, and food allergies.

Review vendor-provided nutrition and allergen details wherever available in the CafeNow Service Portal. If details are missing, contact the vendor.

2. Nutrition Information

Each menu item may display the following nutritional information:

Calories (kcal): Estimated energy provided by the food item.

Protein (g): Amount of protein per serving.

Carbohydrates (g): Total carbohydrate content.

Fat (g): Total fat content.

Sodium (mg): Estimated sodium content.

Sugar (g): Total sugar content.

Employees can review these details before placing an order to select meals that better match their dietary preferences.

3. Allergen Information

CafeNow displays available allergen information based on the ingredients associated with each menu item.

Common allergens and ingredients that employees should look out for include:

Wheat and gluten-containing ingredients

Milk and dairy products

Eggs

Soy

Sesame

Peanuts

Tree nuts

Mustard

Fish and shellfish

Sulphites

Allergen information is displayed on the individual menu item details page wherever available.

4. How to Check Nutrition & Allergen Information

Follow these steps to review food information before ordering:

Log in to the CafeNow Service Portal.

Navigate to the Menu section.

Select your preferred cafeteria vendor.

Browse the available food and beverage items.

Click on a menu item to open its details.

Review the nutritional information, ingredients, and allergen details.

If you have a food allergy or dietary concern, contact the respective vendor before placing your order.

5. Important Food Allergy Disclaimer

Your safety is our priority.

Although CafeNow provides allergen information, food preparation environments may involve shared equipment, utensils, cooking surfaces, storage areas, and preparation facilities.

Cross-contact between ingredients may occur even when an allergen is not listed as a direct ingredient.

Employees with severe food allergies should:

Always inform the vendor about their specific allergy before ordering.

Confirm all ingredients directly with the food vendor.

Ask about shared preparation equipment and potential cross-contact.

Avoid ordering an item if its allergen status cannot be confirmed.

Never rely solely on the information displayed in the application when managing a severe food allergy.

CafeNow cannot guarantee that any menu item is completely free from a particular allergen.

6. Nutrition Data Accuracy

Nutritional values displayed in CafeNow are estimates intended to provide general dietary guidance.

Actual nutritional content may vary depending on:

Portion sizes

Ingredient substitutions

Food preparation methods

Sauces, toppings, and additional ingredients

Vendor-specific recipes

For the most accurate nutritional information, employees should contact the respective food vendor.

7. Reporting Incorrect Information

If you notice missing, outdated, or potentially incorrect nutrition or allergen information:

Contact the cafeteria vendor responsible for the menu item.

Provide the name of the menu item and vendor.

Report the issue to the CafeNow support or administration team.

The administration team can review the reported information and coordinate necessary updates.

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Can I trust the allergen information displayed in CafeNow?

The information is provided as general guidance based on available ingredient data. Always confirm allergens directly with the vendor, especially if you have a severe allergy.

Q2. Does CafeNow guarantee allergen-free meals?

No. CafeNow cannot guarantee that meals are free from allergens or cross-contact.

Q3. Can I check calories before placing an order?

Yes. Where nutritional information is available, you can review calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, sodium, and sugar through the menu item details.

Q4. What should I do if my dietary requirements are not listed?

Contact the vendor directly to confirm whether the menu item meets your dietary requirements.

Q5. Who should I contact if the displayed information is incorrect?

Contact the respective vendor or the CafeNow administration team to report the issue.
