## Application-module story checklist

Here, application modules means navigation entries into CafeNow capabilities. Verify whether each role needs a platform module or an entry point in its portal/workspace; do not assume portal users need the platform navigator.

- [ ] For each role, identify the capabilities it needs to open directly and explain the business purpose.
- [ ] Record the proposed application menu/module label, destination, relevant table or page, intended roles and record scope.
- [ ] Include an acceptance criterion for the intended role finding and opening the entry point.
- [ ] Include an acceptance criterion for an unauthorized role being unable to access the underlying records through a direct link or another route.
- [ ] Check whether an existing module/portal/workspace entry meets the need before proposing another.
- [ ] Keep module visibility and data authorization as separate review items; hiding navigation does not protect records.
- [ ] Review any technical-administration entry points against actual delegated permissions. A reported sys admin role label does not establish platform `admin` or security privileges.

## Access and ACL review checklist

- [ ] Employees can access only their own orders and lines; ownership cannot be supplied or changed to impersonate another employee.
- [ ] Vendor access requires an agreed active vendor-membership mapping, beyond possession of a vendor role.
- [ ] Review line access through its parent order and prevent forged order/vendor/item references.
- [ ] Identify who can change menu prices/availability, order state, vendor/employee references, captured prices and audit/timestamp fields.
- [ ] Define facility-admin business access separately from sys-admin technical access, including reports and sensitive fields.
- [ ] Define delete/archive policy; do not assume every administrator needs routine order deletion.
- [ ] Cover direct reads/writes, portal/workspace actions, reports and any exposed APIs, beyond visible buttons and filters.
- [ ] Retain server-side checks for capacity, availability, transitions and cancellation. Resolve cutoff boundaries and eligible cancellation states before configuring those rules.
